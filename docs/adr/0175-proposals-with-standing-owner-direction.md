# 0175 — Research-backed proposals with standing owner direction

Status: accepted. Date: 2026-10-31.

## Decision

Build proactive work on the existing finite, owner-approved shift. A morning round can collect a declared,
versioned read plan before its single private model request. Marketing's initial plan reads daily site counts
for fourteen complete UTC days and up to five landing pages for the same dates in the analytics property's
calendar: at most two calls and fifteen seconds of collection. Only already-connected integrations are used.
Failures are recorded as unknown. No retries, new connections, publishing, outreach or additional model calls.

The schedule explicitly opts into `marketing-site-signals-v1`. Its definition and authority fingerprint bind
that version. Old schedules omit the field and keep their existing authority. The host provides a read-only,
employee-scoped toolbox to the role; core neither imports an integration nor chooses a role's queries. Research
is sealed into the measured context before admission and remains unverified observation, not shared knowledge.
Authority is checked before collection and again before dispatch. A pause during collection prevents dispatch.

An owner can veto a proposal or correct a result directly. `proposal.feedback` validates the exact source digest
and quoted subject, then saves and approves employee-scoped feedback atomically through the existing memory
store and command receipt. The raw result stays untouched. The source records what prompted the decision;
it is not approved factual evidence. Saving a correction approves the owner's instruction, not the model's claims.
Repeated correction of the same subject explicitly supersedes the previous correction in that transaction.

Every later request for that employee carries **all** current standing decisions, outside lexical retrieval
and the eight-lesson window. They never expire automatically. At twenty-four decisions or eight thousand
subject/statement characters, new decisions are refused until obsolete ones are undone; old vetoes are never
dropped to make room. Changes invalidate pending packets; completed work remains historical evidence. Undo
revokes the decision and starts nothing. Owner-only permissions, company isolation and audit receipts apply.

Exact normalized proposal titles are blocked before dispatch and their Home actions are suppressed. The model
also receives an instruction to avoid equivalent or paraphrased proposals. **Semantic equivalence is not a
deterministic guarantee**: this is not a classifier or an additional model judge, and raw drafts are never rewritten
to conceal a failure. A future semantic guard needs separate evidence and a reviewed design.

## Consequences

The owner can delegate discovery and drafting within reviewed bounds, with persistent corrections and a direct
veto. This does not grant an open-ended agent permission to browse arbitrary sites, spend, contact people or
execute its own proposals. Additional proposals still use the existing one-consent draft path. Existing shifts
must be replaced and reviewed to add automatic source reads; the upgrade does not activate that permission.
Real usefulness and obedience to paraphrased vetoes remain model-quality acceptance gates, separate from
fictional scheduler and browser verification.
