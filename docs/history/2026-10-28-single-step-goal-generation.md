## First raw acceptance result

were supplied: a business sentence, a weekly-content goal and the public website. One checkbox and **Generate my
first draft** produced the result without visiting the business editor, People or a second run-approval screen.
Three public pages were saved. Website lookup start to saved draft took 18.4 seconds; task creation to saved draft
took 12.4 seconds. These are server timestamps, not a full signup-to-value timing study.

Exactly one native Luna/Codex OAuth request produced one raw artifact: 8,119 input and 718 output tokens, with zero
reported reasoning tokens despite the high-effort request. No dollar charge or actual credit consumption was
reported. The artifact remains unchanged, version 1, awaiting review. No editorial copy, repair, second request,
publishing or recurring activation was used for this acceptance run. Private inputs, output and receipts stay ignored.

The workflow passes this local acceptance walkthrough. The raw draft supplies a fictional repair-shop example,
three posts, a schedule, reporting actions and a tally. It respects the distinction between owner facts and website
claims. Usefulness remains unresolved: Friday mostly rewords the sample instead of making a strong improvement,
the same product sentence repeats, and the decision rule leaves exactly one useful completion without a next step.
This result is not evidence that consistently useful first drafts are solved. Owner assessment is pending.
