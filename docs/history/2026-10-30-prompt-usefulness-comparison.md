## Copy-control repair found during the walkthrough

The new plan used plain weekday labels rather than full dates or Markdown headings. Its
individual posts initially lacked copy buttons. The renderer now recognizes repeated standalone
weekdays at the start of a section using the same conservative grouping as full dates.
An isolated weekday, a weekday within prose, labels following introductory prose, and quoted
or fenced labels are not inferred as copy sections. Nested content and whole-plan copying
remain intact; the saved model text is not rewritten.
