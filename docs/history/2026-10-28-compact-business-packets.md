# Compact business packets and raw comparisons

New task packets no longer repeat an approved structured brief inside the legacy company-knowledge
string when that string exactly matches the generated representation. Independently authored knowledge
is retained. Empty, uncited `Unknown` placeholders move into an optional `unfilledFields` list, with
instructions explaining that an unfilled category does not contradict supplied facts elsewhere in the
brief. Meaningful uncertainty, owner-entered values, evidence, freshness and the complete saved profile
remain intact. Earlier captured packets retain their original serialization and digests.

This addresses a concrete contradiction in recent raw drafts: the model cited services or voice from the
business sentence, then called the same category unknown. It adds no extraction pass, reclassification,
authority change or automatic repair. It is a context-composition correction under the existing design.

business inputs as their preceding runs. Exact task input, responsibility, department context, business
fields, profile digest, runtime and execution policy matched. Both new conversations had empty history,
no feedback and one unedited model-output artifact. They are later comparisons, not replacement first-run
