# Grounded actions and ready private drafts

The previous cycle delivered the simpler workspace and exposed two remaining problems: generated copy can
infer operational details, and Home labels finished private drafts as work that demands owner intervention.

## Raw usefulness evidence

Marketing instructions now distinguish creative wording from business operations, preserve established
customer routes, and explain that a review-note caveat cannot justify an unsupported public claim. New
employee setup proposes an audience when absent rather than instructing the employee to ask immediately.

Two native Luna/Codex OAuth requests used the standing allowance: high effort, a 3,000-output-token target,
90-second local deadline and one intended existing credit each, all soft limits. Both completed without
retry. No previous output or feedback was supplied, and no assistant editorial artifacts were created.

Both reported zero reasoning tokens; actual credit charges were not returned. The allowance ledger now
contains seven completed requests out of 31, leaving 24. These seven reported 44,385 input + output tokens.
Across previously audited application evaluation batches: 22 requests / 166,161 reported tokens. These
figures exclude the coding assistant's conversation usage. Exact packets, raw bodies and receipts stay
private under `.pilot/goal-start/`.

Adding more instruction text has not reliably removed the operational assumptions. Further calls should
answer a specific new question; these results do not justify a claimed success rate or another rescue edit.

## Ready drafts without false urgency

Home, team status and the cockpit distinguish finished private text drafts from items needing action.
Eligible drafts show **Ready to use**, appear in **Drafts ready**, and no longer inflate the cockpit's
attention badge. Owners can open, copy or refine them directly. Their stored approval state is unchanged.
Home's activity counters and unpriced-job accounting are collapsed together so they do not dominate a
finished result. The result, next goal and employee remain visible.

This applies only to normal-priority reviews of committed, unedited model results in private standalone
work. Shared context, schedules, plans, meetings, resource jobs, dependency-linked tasks, requested edits,
unresolved execution and consequential authorization items remain actionable. The result's approval,
handoff and schedule controls retain their existing requirements.

## Verification

Web and Marketing builds and existing Marketing routing/check scripts pass. Focused classification checks
cover the private-result case and exclusion boundaries. Installed Home and cockpit walkthroughs verify
ready-result navigation and the absence of a false attention badge. Both apps retain fresh backups;
saved raw outputs, accounts and schedule records are preserved. No new ADR: this changes presentation and
drafting guidance, not authorization or execution architecture.

Owner usefulness assessment and prospective-customer validation remain open. The full three-phase goal
is not complete; this cycle improves immediate use and records a limit of prompt-only quality tuning.
