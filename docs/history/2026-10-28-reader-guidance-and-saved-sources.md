# Reader guidance and readable saved sources

The owner rejected the raw solo-alterations plan for unedited use. It repeated the business description
and caveats, offered shallow advice, labeled public calls to action as internal instructions, relied on
unrequested attribution, and added unnecessary preparation work. This cycle changes the Marketing
instructions and observes two new raw comparisons; it does not repair either result with editorial copies.

## Changes

- Company claims and commitments must come from approved context. Ordinary practical reader guidance may
  use domain knowledge, provided it does not invent company services, policies, requirements or promises.
- Weekly copy asks for useful substance, natural calls to action, relevant qualifications rather than
  repeated footers, and attribution requests in the actual posts when needed for the learning signal.
- Preparation covers necessary remaining work, excluding unrequested photo sourcing, document copying,
  reconfirming known facts and daily approval chores. The same instructions reach the Home assignment.
- Draft Source and provenance now lazily displays the exact saved business context: values, approval date,
  verification status, source excerpts and captured freshness. Public website material remains distinctly
  unapproved. The existing task review uses this readable view as well.
- The source read is bound to company, attempt, task, digest and artifact context. A mismatching response is
  refused; a late response cannot populate another company or result. Read failures offer a read-only reload.
  Existing consent, sharing and scheduling gates are unchanged.
