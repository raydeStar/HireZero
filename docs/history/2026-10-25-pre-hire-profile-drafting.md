# Pre-hire profile drafting and first Marketing plan

Scope remains all three phases in [the delivery ledger](../CONTEXT_FIRST_DELIVERY.md). This receipt advances the
context-first onboarding journey; it does not claim a completed product phase or a live deployment.

## Implemented

- A role-neutral company draft request captures the confirmed identity, up to three saved sources, existing owner
  corrections and an exact measured-provider allowance. No hidden employee or department is created.
- The owner reviews and authorizes one request. Its company budget reservation survives unknown outcomes and
  restart. Replays return saved receipts. Explicit reconciliation requires usage, a receipt, evidence and confirmed
  termination; known overruns are recorded without increasing the budget limit.
- Structured responses require all nine fields and exact quotes from selected company sources. Models cannot
  claim owner provenance. Invalid, stale and late output is withheld. Existing owner corrections survive extraction;
  unsaved browser edits survive loading a generated brief. Approval remains a separate exact action.
- Native and API adapters accept the explicit pre-hire measured policy. Fixed two integration mistakes found during
  regression: requiring an employee for a company-only native packet, and confusing automatic qualification with
  the existing explicit one-job capability. Automatic execution remains disabled for both real adapters.
- Marketing contributes suggested setup from an approved brief and a concrete one-week starter-plan request.
  Department defaults reference shared company facts rather than copying claims into independently approved records.
  The first request uses the existing private conversation approval flow and persists its response across reloads.
- The first request pins the company knowledge it was based on. Changed or revoked knowledge cannot be substituted
  while preparing it, and revocation invalidates an already prepared packet.
