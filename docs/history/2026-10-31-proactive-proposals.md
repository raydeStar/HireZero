# Proactive proposals and persistent owner feedback — 2026-10-31

The owner asked for employees that find useful work, do the permitted preparation, and bring proposals with
minimal steering, while remembering corrections and vetoes. [ADR 0175](../adr/0175-proposals-with-standing-owner-direction.md)
records the new authority and persistence boundaries.

## Delivered

- New Marketing shifts can opt into two bounded, read-only checks of already-connected site activity before
  each morning's single model request. Fourteen complete days of daily counts and five landing pages inform
  the proposal; unavailable data is unknown, and collection stops after fifteen seconds. No discovery loops,
  new connections, publishing, outreach or additional model requests.
- Each morning job has **Don't suggest this again**. **That's inaccurate** opens a short correction form on
  the report and on saved drafts. Saving is one atomic owner decision; raw model output is unchanged.
- Standing directions survive reloads, later rounds and the ordinary eight-lesson window. Exact normalized
  titles cannot dispatch while vetoed. Undo starts nothing. Reworded equivalents depend on model obedience;
  this is not a proven semantic classifier.
- A pending request becomes stale when its employee's standing directions change. Completed historical work
  remains separate. Decisions are employee-scoped and visible under Remembered decisions, with no expiry.
- What the employee checked is available in a collapsed disclosure. The proposed work stays foremost.
