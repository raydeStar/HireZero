# UX audit and design direction (2026-10-20)

The owner handed the user experience over for an enterprise-quality redesign, after the Muse layout returned
([ADR 0040](../adr/0040-the-muse-layout.md)). This note records what a walk through every screen found, at 1440×900
with fictional data, the direction for fixing it, and the order of the work.

## What the walk found

The pattern behind most findings: **the app shows its safety machinery before the job.** Allowances, native turns,
evidence and authority statements come first, and what the owner came to do comes second. The same information also
appears in several places at once.

1. **The composer buries the message.** In an employee chat, these all stack between the conversation and the message
   box, taking about half the screen:
   - Work type;
   - Message action;
   - Intended reply credit allowance;
   - a paragraph about native turns and soft stops;
   - Actions;
   - Attach approved evidence.
2. **Three layers of header.** Above every chat sit:
   - the top bar;
   - a block with the employee's responsibility, a capability sentence, a conversation picker and "New conversation";
   - a "Conversation context and employee settings" disclosure.
3. **The home is a chat you cannot chat in.** "Company conversation" opens with a legal paragraph ("The human owner
   retains final authority…"), and its composer defaults to "Reply as: Save owner note only".
4. **The sidebar is uneven:**
   - employee entries are indented differently from one another;
   - "Hire or manage employees" and "Today, budgets, members and settings" look like body text;
   - an authority disclaimer sits between them.
5. **People opens on a hiring form,** not on the team.
6. **Today repeats the cockpit,** with a full-width priority dropdown and a full-width Snooze button under every item.
7. **Names that need translating:**
   - "Company" in the rail next to "Company conversation";
   - "Board" for meetings;
   - "Approve content" for approving a result.
8. **Visual rhythm:**
   - long reports dominate the chat;
   - there are many small font sizes;
   - collapsed `<details>` arrows are used as the main way to reveal things.

## Direction

- **Job first, safety one step away.** The message box and its send button come first. Options sit in one compact row
  under it. The explanation of how a reply is authorized is one click away, never in the way. Every safety check
  stays exactly where it is enforced: on the server and on the job card.
- **One home for each thing:**
  - what needs you lives in the cockpit;
  - the day's history lives in Today;
  - settings live in the Company hub;
  - the team lives in People.
- **Calm, plain language.** No legal paragraphs in headers. Short labels and verbs that say what happens: "Approve",
  "Ask for changes", "Prepare a reply".
- **Consistent, scannable lists:**
  - the same row anatomy (avatar or icon, title, one muted line, status) in the sidebar, the cockpit, the board and
    People;
  - buttons look like buttons;
  - one indentation rule.
- **Progressive disclosure through panels,** not long pages: actions and evidence open as panels above the composer,
  and hiring opens from a button.

## Order of work

1. **Conversation:**
   - a compact composer, with options in one row and Actions and Evidence as panels;
   - a one-line conversation header;
   - a calm empty state.
2. **Shell:**
   - a consistent sidebar, with initials, a status dot and clear Hire and Settings buttons;
   - "Board" renamed "Meetings".
3. **People:** the team first, hiring from a button.
4. **Today:** compact rows with inline priority and snooze.
5. **Next rounds:**
   - a real Home overview, with the team's status and recent results;
   - reports shown as summaries with "show full report";
   - a type scale and spacing pass;
   - keyboard shortcuts;
   - light-theme review.

## Done so far

- **Round 1 (feature/ux-round-1):**
  - the compact composer and the one-line conversation header;
  - the sidebar's rows and foot actions, and "Meetings" in the rail;
  - People showing the team first;
  - compact Today rows.
- **Round 2 (feature/ux-round-2):**
  - **Home is the landing page.** It has tiles (Needs you, which opens the first item; In progress; Up next; Done;
    Spent to date), team cards whose status line comes from real results needing review, and recent results with
    state chips, each opening its work window.
  - **Long reports show their opening lines** with a fade and "Show full report". The full text stays in the page.
  - **The review buttons say "Approve result" and "Ask for changes".**
