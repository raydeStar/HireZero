# Fresh generated brief and replacement review

## Result

One fresh native website-brief request completed successfully after ADR 0158. It used the three previously captured
public sources and preserved the exact owner goal. The generated nine-field profile is a separate, unapproved
draft. The previously approved editorial brief, employee knowledge, tasks and artifacts remain unchanged.

This is the first successful fresh native extraction in this website pilot after the earlier quotation and owner
attribution failures. It establishes one functioning real path, not reliable quality across businesses or owner
acceptance of the new draft.

## Output assessment and usage

- All five quotations match the captured source text exactly after canonicalization.
- Four fields describe website claims or messaging; one opportunity remains explicitly a hypothesis.
- Customers, market and competitors remain Unknown. The owner goal retains owner provenance with no citation.
- The claims were checked against the captured pages. Some short quotations support only part of their field's
  wording; the surrounding page supports the additional detail. Exact quotation matching alone is not a semantic
  fact-check or independent verification of advertised capabilities.
- One Luna request through Codex OAuth reported 8,611 input and 468 output tokens, 9,079 total, with zero reported
  reasoning tokens. Actual subscription-credit consumption was not supplied. The business-profile policy used its
  existing default effort, a 3,000-output-token target, 120-second local deadline and intended allowance of one
  existing credit, all soft limits. This was not a high-effort Marketing deliverable run.
- No retry, historical response reprocessing, publication or recurring activation occurred. Private request,
  response and comparison receipts remain outside Git.

## UX finding and correction

The new draft offered approval while another brief was still approved. The server correctly refused replacement
until explicit withdrawal, but the required control was hidden in another section. Under
[ADR 0160](../adr/0160-clear-business-brief-replacement.md), the draft now explains which version employees use,
disables premature approval, and opens and focuses the current approved brief for review. Withdrawal and approval
remain separate actions.
