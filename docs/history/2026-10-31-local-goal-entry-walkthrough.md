# Local goal-to-first-draft walkthrough

Home's goal form sat below shift, results and planning cards. First-time users also
had three explanation tiles to pass before reaching an input. The form now follows
the greeting, with a short introduction and less empty space. Existing result and
shift links remain available below it.

An opened draft keeps unrelated company-attention prompts out of its reading area.
Running work remains visible, and company decisions remain in the inbox and cockpit.
Refine focuses and reveals its feedback field; closing it returns focus to the
trigger. The example feedback no longer suggests inventing an offer. Draft copy now
states that the result is already saved and can be used before optional final approval.

The native run exposed another issue: an italic inline `Review notes:` label was
not recognized by the presentation splitter. Those internal notes appeared in the
email and its copy/download output. The splitter now recognizes matching italic or
bold markers for that inline label. Notes are collapsed, while the complete raw
response remains available through **Download full saved result**. Stored artifacts,
their digests, prompts and approval rules are unchanged.

- The isolated Home browser journey covers desktop and mobile, one Home consent,
  optional website discovery, automatic specialist setup, disclosure failure,
  lost-response recovery and returning to the latest revision after reload.
  Added checks cover first-input visibility, refinement focus/return, clean copy
  and draft download, and the unchanged full-result download. These fixtures make
  no model calls or external requests.
- One separately owner-approved native Home request used a fresh fictional
  book-cover studio, its business sentence and a welcome-email goal. Website was
  blank. Generate prepared and dispatched the request, then opened the draft
  automatically without another consent or run action.
- The untouched email supplies five fill-in prompts, a complete mystery-cover
  example and a natural reply invitation. Its approximately 210-word body fits the
  requested 180–230-word range. No invented prices, booking routes, turnaround
  promises or free consultation appear. Agent assessment: useful as a first draft;
  owner usefulness acceptance remains separate. The review-note presentation bug
  is recorded above, rather than concealed by an editorial copy.
- The sole native request completed through Luna/Codex OAuth at high effort under
  v3 terms: 6,160 input tokens and 907 reported output tokens, including 516 reasoning
  tokens. Actual subscription-credit charges are unavailable. No retry, revision
  run, publishing, external outreach or assistant-authored artifact copy occurred.
- Private raw output, request/receipt, screenshots and install records are retained
  outside source control. The frontend build and repository formatters pass;
  Vite retains its existing large-chunk advisory.

Both local web roots received hash-verified backups and verified asset copies, with
the entry document copied last and earlier assets retained for already-open tabs.
No host restart or data migration was needed. Readback of the existing company
schedule retained its digest, version, active state, current review and zero consumed
occurrences. This cycle does not change its spending authority.
