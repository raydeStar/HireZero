# Small-team draft experience and raw persona evaluation

The owner redirected first-use quality toward ordinary businesses, authorized up to six additional native Luna
evaluations, and required every first output to remain unedited. This cycle uses two new requests plus the
previously authorized welcome-email result. No editorial artifacts, retries, publication or external actions.

## Product changes

- Weekly starter requests and role instructions now let the business and goal determine the content mix,
  reader action and learning signal. Removed the prescribed three-stage workflow demonstration. Complete
  assets and review notes have room for roughly 750–900 words, with shorter owner requests taking precedence.
- Home results lead with draft text and Copy, Download and Refine. Provenance, original request, execution
  receipt, costs, sharing and task administration are collapsed. The blank editorial form no longer fills
  the result view. Reading/copying needs no approval; shared context, handoffs and schedules retain their gates.
- Primary navigation is Home, People and Company, with a persistent Settings shortcut. Company knowledge
  groups Business brief, Documents and Memory. Administrative views live under Settings / Advanced.
  Plans and Meetings remain secondary until multiple employees or a CEO are present; existing deep links work.
- Draft length guidance matches the expanded ceiling. Numbered posts and other weekdays are recognized.
  Manual review hints no longer accumulate into a misleading defect count.

## Verification and limits

Production web and Marketing builds passed; 56 focused goal, revision and schedule facts passed, as did
Marketing routing and draft-check tests. The installed UI showed raw results opening automatically, grouped
navigation and collapsed details. Refine opened without dispatch. Download matched the saved raw email
exactly. Copy reported success, but the browser clipboard reader returned empty, so clipboard fidelity was
not verified through that interface.

Both local installations have verified backups; account state, saved work and schedule records were retained.
Changing Marketing role instructions invalidates the existing weekly schedule's authority fingerprint.
Its stored state can still say active before its due-time check, but it needs a newly reviewed schedule before
it can run under these instructions. No replacement was activated by this evaluation authorization.

The first-run content gate remains open. Follow-up direct-asset comparisons will be recorded separately;
they cannot replace these first-run findings or establish owner/customer acceptance.
