# Reader-purpose candidate: mixed results, rolled back

The latest raw alterations plan repeated caveats and treated useful advice before purchasing an alteration
as advice before buying new clothes. Its exact request also ended with a broad Core instruction against
inferring unsupplied facts, despite the role explicitly permitting ordinary professional guidance.

A two-case candidate clarified company-fact grounding, asked for distinct reader questions, allowed actions
other than enquiries, and requested a concrete learning tally. The candidate changed both shared wording and
role guidance; these observations cannot isolate the contribution of either change.

## Raw comparisons

Both requests ran once from the installed Home flow through native Luna/Codex OAuth. Approved business facts,
department context, owner goals and model policies matched the preceding comparisons. No earlier output or
feedback entered either request. Each produced one unchanged version-one model artifact.

| Case | What improved | What still failed | Assessment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Solo alterations | Correct audience situation; more concrete learning tally | Invitation to enquire about unlisted work, repeated scope/pricing caveats, internal approval language in customer copy, repeated advice and awkward attribution | Not usable as written |
| Three-person agency | Completed worked examples, distinct reader actions and a concrete tally | Redundant comment attribution, generic review chores and nonessential owner decisions | Stronger sample by assistant assessment; no owner acceptance |

The candidate was not retained. Source and both local installations were restored to the preceding prompt
baseline. The exact pre-experiment Core and Marketing assemblies were restored from verified backups; the
completed-message transport fix remains installed. The better agency sample does not establish a reliable
improvement across businesses, and the failed alterations output stays in the evidence record.

## Delivered UX correction

results. Home says the draft can be read, copied or refined. This describes availability without implying a
quality verdict. Copy, Download and Refine remain immediate; no new approval step was added.
