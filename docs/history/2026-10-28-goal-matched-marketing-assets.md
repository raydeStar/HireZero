# Marketing work follows the requested goal

Home previously sent every Marketing goal through a one-week, three-post starter request. An owner asking for a
welcome email or homepage copy could therefore receive a different deliverable despite the original goal being
present in the packet.

Marketing now supplies a direct goal-draft skill using the existing measured-deliverable policy and consent flow.
The request preserves the owner's format, count, audience and constraints and leads with the finished asset.
Explicit content-plan/calendar requests retain the weekly-plan path. A broad marketing goal asks for one proposed
useful asset rather than imposing a campaign. Role selection now recognizes an explicit email request.

The new skill uses the existing role module contract. Core and integrations are unchanged; the existing starter-plan
instructions and approved schedules retain their prior bindings. Weekly-plan checks are not applied to emails or
other direct assets. No architectural decision changed, so no ADR was added.

Focused goal-selection checks cover homepage copy, a welcome email, one post, an explicitly rejected weekly plan,
a broad sales goal and three content-plan requests. Forty-eight backend facts pass, covering the exact prepared
request, deliverable terms, assignments, retained revisions and schedules. The Home browser journey now includes
a change of specialist and a direct email: one consent, one fictional run, one raw result opened automatically.
Production builds and an isolated staged-host compatibility check also pass. No live model call was made.

The existing weekly-drafting browser fixture originally relied on the old behavior: its broad launch goal always
produced a weekly plan. The regression now requests a weekly content plan explicitly and follows the revised setup
copy. The distinct direct-asset behavior is covered by the new Home and goal-selection checks.
The updated weekly-drafting journey passes, including generated-brief approval, exact consent, result review,
revision history and uncertain-operation recovery.

Both local installations have application/data recovery backups and verified file hashes. Only the Marketing
module and web files changed; connector/runtime binaries are unchanged. Accounts, work, approved schedules and
audit chains are preserved. The installed direct and weekly paths offer identical measured terms. The raw
acceptance workspace's task, artifact, measured and knowledge records compare unchanged after installation.

This establishes routing and execution behavior with fictional providers. Raw model quality and owner usefulness
remain separate, open acceptance gates; the earlier real first result is not rewritten or replaced.
