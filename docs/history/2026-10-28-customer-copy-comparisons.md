# Consolidated customer-copy instructions

The preceding cycle allowed useful domain guidance, but raw outputs still repeated caveats, invented a
contact route or placed internal pricing status in customer copy. Inspection of the exact saved requests
showed weekly-plan guidance repeated in the role instructions and the Home assignment. A generic request
for reversible assumptions did not prevent the model from treating an operational route as a creative choice.

## Change and scope

The weekly role now presents one organised set of instructions for customer copy, using the week and internal
review notes. The Home assignment preserves the exact owner goal and delegates the detailed format guidance
to the role. This consolidates existing requirements rather than adding another full instruction layer.

Customer copy must keep internal setup/approval/pricing metadata out, use known contact routes, avoid inviting
unlisted services and ask an answerable public question when no contact route is supplied. Wording and days
are creative choices; operations remain facts. Practical domain advice is still allowed. The requested
deliverables, length, distinct useful takeaways, complete worked assets, natural CTAs, light preparation,
collectable learning signal and factual grounding remain required. No model policy, runtime pin, authority
boundary or automatic repair loop changed.

The two matched application packets shrank from 10,776 to 8,913 bytes and from 10,859 to 8,996 bytes.
Reported input tokens fell from 6,213 to 5,862 and from 6,234 to 5,886 respectively. This is a measured reduction
in these packets; it does not establish that shorter prompts caused any quality change.
